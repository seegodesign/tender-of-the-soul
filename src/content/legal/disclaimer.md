## Not medical or crisis care

Services and website content are not a substitute for medical care, psychiatric care, psychotherapy, crisis support, diagnosis, or treatment from an appropriately licensed professional. Tender of the Soul does not provide emergency or crisis services.

## No guarantees

Descriptions on this site reflect possibilities and intentions, not guarantees. Individual experiences vary, and no specific therapeutic, medical, emotional, or spiritual outcome is promised.

## Educational content

Journal articles and other site materials are for general educational purposes and should not be treated as individualized advice.
